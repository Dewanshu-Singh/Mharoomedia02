Onboarding a Client Is a Challenge. Retaining One Is a Whole New Game.

A pitch may open the door. The work that follows defines the partnership. Discover how Mharoo Media approaches higher education client retention through diagnosis, structured execution, and measurable conversion outcomes.

At Mharoo Media, we believe a strategy should address an institution's specific needs rather than copy competitors or follow digital trends.

Onboarding a new client is a challenge. You have to understand their expectations, earn their confidence, and prove that your team is the right fit for the job. But retaining a client is a whole new game.

It requires more than a convincing pitch. It requires a clear strategy, consistent execution, and the ability to adapt when things do not go as planned.

At Mharoo Media, this month has brought us two new educational institution partnerships and the opportunity to work again with one of our most valued clients, SGVU (Suresh Gyan Vihar University).

These milestones are exciting, but they also raise an important question: What happens after a client signs with an agency?

For us, that is where the real work begins.

The Retention Philosophy "A pitch may open the door. The work that follows defines the partnership."

1. Understanding Before Executing

Every educational institution has a different story, audience, and set of challenges.

Some universities have excellent infrastructure and academic facilities but struggle to communicate their strengths online. Others have a strong reputation but need to reach more prospective students or improve the quality of their admission enquiries.

That is why our first step is to understand the institution before suggesting solutions.

We look closely at its courses, target audience, digital presence, competitors, strengths, and admission goals. We also identify what makes the university different from other institutions offering similar programmes.

Pillar 01
Courses & Core USPs

Identifying flagship programmes and what sets academic facilities apart from peers.

Pillar 02
Audience & Aspirations

Profiling student personas, geographic zones, and parental decision priorities.

Pillar 03
Competitive Landscape

Benchmarking narrative positioning against regional and national universities.

Pillar 04
Real Admission Targets

Moving past vanity metrics to concrete seat intake and enrolment milestones.

Instead of immediately asking, "What should we post?", we begin with a more important question:

"What does this university need to achieve?"

The answer shapes the strategy that follows.

2. Finding the Real Problem

Not every marketing challenge can be solved by posting more reels or increasing the advertising budget.

If students are unaware of a university, the priority may be to build visibility. If they know about it but are not considering it, the institution may need stronger storytelling, clearer communication, and more reasons for prospective students to trust it.

Similarly, if advertisements generate enquiries but few students move towards applications, increasing the number of leads may not solve the actual problem.

The Institutional Gap Diagnostic

Scenario A
Students unaware of university existence?
The true need is hyper-targeted brand discovery, campus awareness, and search visibility.
Scenario B
Students aware, but not considering applying?
The institution needs authentic student proof, faculty credibility, placement showcases, and campus lifestyle storytelling.
Scenario C
High leads generated, but flat admission numbers?
Pumping more budget into lead generation will only burn capital. The real fix is admission funnel counseling alignment, lead qualification, and nurturing.

The challenge is to identify where the gap exists.

At Mharoo Media, we believe a strategy should address an institution's specific needs rather than copy competitors or follow every new digital trend. Understanding the problem helps us choose the right tools and avoid spending resources on activities that do not serve the larger goal.

3. Turning Promises Into a Plan

A good presentation can create excitement, but a presentation alone cannot deliver results.

Once we understand an institution's goals, we develop a practical plan. This includes identifying the target audience, choosing the right marketing channels, planning content, establishing timelines, and deciding how progress will be measured.

Depending on the university's requirements, this multi-faceted execution ecosystem may involve:

Every activity needs a purpose. A reel should do more than fill a content calendar, and an advertisement should do more than spend a budget. Each effort should contribute to a larger objective, whether that means building awareness, reaching prospective students, or generating relevant admission enquiries.

Clear expectations are equally important. Both the institution and the agency should understand the deliverables, responsibilities, timelines, and performance indicators from the beginning.

This creates a shared direction and makes it easier to evaluate whether the strategy is moving forward as planned.

4. Telling the University's Story

A university is much more than its buildings, classrooms, and course brochures. It is the student discovering a new interest, the professor inspiring a career choice, and the campus experience that shapes a student's journey.

These stories help an institution connect with prospective students on an emotional and aspirational level.

Our approach is to communicate a university's identity through authentic content, including:

  • Student Experiences: Day-in-the-life vlogs, real campus journeys, student triumphs, and community societies.
  • Faculty Insights: Research breakthroughs, passionate professorial mentorship, and classroom energy.
  • Campus Life: Cultural fests, sports tournaments, labs, libraries, hostel communities, and dining culture.
  • Academic Achievements & Industry Exposure: Live industry consulting, hackathons, incubator launches, and multinational campus placement milestones.

However, creative storytelling must work alongside performance marketing. While content helps build awareness and interest, targeted campaigns can reach students actively exploring their educational options.

The goal is not simply to make a university look attractive online. It is to help the right audience understand what it offers and why it deserves consideration.

Views, likes, and followers are easy to notice, but they do not tell the whole story. For an educational institution, meaningful progress also means reaching the right audience, generating relevant enquiries, and improving campaign performance.

This is where data becomes important.

The objective is not simply to generate activity. It is to understand what that activity contributes to the client's goals.
