How Can Universities Use Instagram to Increase Admissions?

A student may never visit your campus before forming an opinion about it. Discover how to turn social attention into verified student admissions.

Mharoo Media Growth Team
University Marketing Specialists
|
October 2026
|
6 Min Read
Prospective students don't want a notice board—they want to see what life at your university could actually look like.

A student may never visit your campus before forming an opinion about it.

They might see your campus on Instagram. Watch a student Reel. Check your faculty. Look at placement stories. Browse your Stories. Read comments. And only then decide whether your university is worth exploring further.

For universities, Instagram is no longer just a social media platform. It is part of the admission journey.

The Problem with the Digital Notice Board Many universities still use Instagram like a digital notice board: admission announcements, event posters, festival pictures, and achievement graphics. The problem? Students don't want to follow a notice board. They want to see what life at your university could actually look like.

So, how can universities use Instagram to turn attention into admissions? Here are the 5 proven pillars.

1. Sell the Experience, Not Just the Course

When universities market a BBA, BTech, or MBA programme, the focus is often on the course itself. But students aren't only choosing a degree.

They're choosing a campus, a community, opportunities, friendships, experiences, and a version of themselves they hope to become.

Notice Board Approach (What Fails)
  • Posting generic graphics: "Admissions Open for BBA"
  • Static flyer designs with excessive text and course bullet points
  • Impersonal corporate branding that fails to spark emotional connection
Experience Approach (What Converts)
  • A day in the life of an active BBA student
  • Students collaborating on an actual industry project
  • Behind-the-scenes at a business pitch competition
  • Live reels from internships and industry visits
  • Student clubs, hackathons, and campus activities
  • What a typical day genuinely feels like
The difference is simple: Don't just tell students what they can study. Show them what they can become.

2. Let Students Tell the Story

A university can say: "We have an amazing campus culture."

But a student saying: "Here's what my first month at university actually felt like" is infinitely more relatable, authentic, and persuasive.

Student-generated content (UGC) can become one of the strongest assets in an education marketing strategy. Universities can build recurring content series around:

High-Performing Student Content Pillars:

Day in the life videos
Student takeovers
Internship journeys
Placement experiences
Hostel & campus life
Club & society stories
Why I chose this university
First-year experiences
Alumni career journeys

This isn't about creating perfectly scripted testimonials. It's about showing prospective students real people they can relate to.

Because a student considering your university isn't only asking: "Is this university good?" They are also asking: "Can I see myself here?"

Your existing students can help answer that question better than a glossy brochure ever could.

3. Use Reels to Get Discovered

For universities, Reels shouldn't be treated simply as viral entertainment. They can be a powerful, algorithmic discovery tool.

The key is to create differentiated content tailored to each stage of the student's decision-making journey:

Top of Funnel

Discovery Content

Designed to reach students who may not know your university yet.

  • 5 things to know before choosing a university
  • POV: Your first day at college
  • What nobody tells you about hostel life
  • BBA vs BCom: Which one should you choose?
Middle of Funnel

Consideration Content

Designed to help interested students evaluate your university.

  • Unfiltered campus tours
  • Faculty introductions & insights
  • High-tech lab walkthroughs
  • Student project highlights
  • Placement success stories
  • Industry collaborations
Bottom of Funnel

Conversion Content

Designed to drive direct action and applications.

  • Admission deadline alerts
  • Scholarship eligibility guides
  • Step-by-step application walkthroughs
  • 1-on-1 counselling booking links
  • Course FAQs & fee breakdowns
  • Campus visit open-house invitations
The Golden Formula: Discovery creates attention. Content builds consideration. A clear Call-to-Action drives admission.

4. Turn Your Faculty and Campus Into Content

Two of the biggest assets universities already have are often underused on social media: their people and their spaces.

Don't just post a stiff photograph of a professor with the generic caption: "Meet our distinguished faculty." Give the professor something useful and engaging to say.

Example Prompt Ideas for Faculty:
• "Three things I would tell every first-year marketing student."
• "What AI skills will actually matter to employers in the next five years?"

The same principle applies to physical infrastructure:

  • Don't simply show an AI lab: Show what students are building inside it.
  • Don't just show the library: Show how students use it during finals week.
  • Don't just show the sports complex: Show the energy of students training there.
The fundamental question should shift from: "What facilities do we have?" to: "What can a student achieve because we have these facilities?" That's the difference between listing features and communicating real value.

5. Connect Instagram to the Admission Funnel

This is where social media transforms into serious marketing.

A Reel getting 100,000 views looks impressive on a dashboard. But views alone don't fill classrooms. Universities must connect social content with the larger, quantifiable admission journey:

The Complete University Admission Journey

How social attention turns into verified campus enrollments

Every single stage in this sequence must have a clear purpose. Rather than tracking superficial likes, growth-focused universities track hard commercial metrics:

Profile Visits

Cost Per Lead (CPL)

The most important question isn't: "How many people watched our Reel?"
It is: "Did the right student take the next step?"
That shift from vanity metrics to admission outcomes is what turns Instagram from a content channel into a formidable growth engine.

Your Instagram Should Feel Like a Window Into Campus

Students don't choose universities because of one viral Instagram post. They build a perception over time.

They see the campus. They meet the students. They hear from faculty. They discover opportunities. They watch student experiences. And gradually, they decide whether they can see themselves there.

That's why university Instagram strategy shouldn't be about posting more. It should be about communicating better.

The Five Core Imperatives:

• Show the experience. Move past brochure talk.
• Tell real stories. Let genuine students speak.
• Answer real questions. Address doubts, costs, and career paths.
• Build trust. Authenticity beats staged corporate polish.
• Make the next step easy. Clear links to counselling and admissions.

Because the ultimate goal isn't simply to make your university visible. It is to make it memorable, credible, and ultimately, chosen.
